# Catetin brand assets

Approved identity: conversational lowercase `c` with three audio-rhythm bars and a Petrona SemiBold 600 wordmark.

## Core colors

- Navy: `#172F49`
- Ivory: `#FAF9F6`
- Powder blue: `#CFE2F1`

## Usage

- `catetin-lockup.svg`: primary horizontal logo on light or transparent surfaces.
- `catetin-lockup-reversed.svg`: horizontal logo on navy/dark surfaces.
- `catetin-mark.svg`: primary standalone mark.
- `catetin-mark-reversed.svg`: standalone mark on navy/dark surfaces.
- `catetin-mark-monochrome.svg`: single-color standalone mark.
- PNG files are raster exports of the canonical SVG artwork.
- `../icons/` contains favicon, Apple touch, PWA, and maskable exports.

Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the width of one internal rhythm bar. Use the standalone mark below widths where the wordmark becomes difficult to read. Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, outline, or add effects.

The wordmark glyphs are converted to vector outlines from Petrona SemiBold 600, so the production SVG does not require the font to be installed.
