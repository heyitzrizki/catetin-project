# Catetin Brand Foundation Design

## Intent

Create a distinctive, production-ready Catetin identity that feels calm, editorial, warm, and modern. The identity must connect conversation with transcription without relying on generic microphone, headphone, cloud, or AI imagery.

## Approved Direction

- Mark: a lowercase `c` whose silhouette also reads as a restrained conversation bubble.
- Interior detail: three vertical audio-rhythm bars.
- Wordmark: lowercase `catetin` set in Petrona SemiBold 600 with optical kerning.
- Palette: navy `#172F49`, ivory `#FAF9F6`, powder blue `#CFE2F1`.
- The mark must remain legible at favicon size and work in a single color.
- Petrona is introduced for the wordmark only. Existing DM Serif Display editorial headings, Plus Jakarta Sans UI/body text, and JetBrains Mono metadata remain unchanged.

## Deliverables

- Standalone mark and horizontal mark-plus-wordmark lockup.
- Navy-on-transparent, ivory/reversed, and monochrome variants.
- Self-contained SVG source assets.
- PNG logo exports, favicon sizes, PWA icons, and a social/OG image.
- Replacement of the current header/footer wordmark and legacy app icon without changing surrounding layout.

## Constraints

- Do not use generated raster artwork as the production logo source.
- Do not redesign the landing page, dashboard, or Studio.
- Preserve accessible text naming for the brand.
- Keep all production assets local; no runtime font or image request to a third-party CDN.
- Include the Petrona license notice required by its distribution terms.

## Approaches Considered

1. Paper plus waveform: immediately understandable but too close to generic document/audio icons.
2. Conversational `c`: selected because it is compact, ownable, favicon-friendly, and expresses both spoken conversation and the Catetin name.
3. Editorial hybrid: flexible but too visually busy at small sizes.
